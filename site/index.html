    Before you install

    This is a new app, so it's not 'signed' yet, Windows may warn you, and VirusTotal may freak out 😭. The code’s all open source, so you can check it yourself if you're worried: github.com/lachydotmcg/capri-desktop. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. :) Need help after that? See the setup guide or ask in the Discord.

    Mac is a preview: she sits on your windows, chats, has her full Mainframe and Mods, and talks through your own speech server or a cloud voice. Apple hasn't approved her yet, so macOS blocks her the first time. Unzip her, drag Capri into Applications, then open Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Capri.app once. That only clears the downloaded-from-the-internet flag on Capri. Her brain runs on Ollama: install it, then run ollama pull gemma4:e4b. Needs macOS 15 or newer. Stuck? Ask in the Discord.

    Linux is a preview: she sits on your windows, chats, has her full Mainframe and Mods, and talks through your own speech server or a cloud voice. Unpack it and run Capri/Capri. Her brain runs on Ollama: install it, then run ollama pull gemma4:e4b. She sits on windows on X11; on Wayland she sits on the bottom of your screen. Stuck? Ask in the Discord.

    Linux is a preview: she sits on your windows, chats, has her full Mainframe and Mods, and talks through your own speech server or a cloud voice. The AppImage is one file with nothing to unpack: tick "Allow executing file as program" in its Properties, or run chmod +x Capri-linux-x64.AppImage, then open it. Her brain runs on Ollama: install it, then run ollama pull gemma4:e4b. She sits on windows on X11; on Wayland she sits on the bottom of your screen. Stuck? Ask in the Discord.

    Capri VR is Capri Room, fadievr's mixed-reality version of her for Meta Quest 3 and 3S. She walks around your room and sits on your furniture. It's not on the Meta store, so you sideload it: turn on Developer Mode for your headset in the Meta Horizon phone app (it needs a free Meta developer account), then install the APK from your PC with SideQuest or Meta Quest Developer Hub. In the headset, open Library > Unknown Sources, then Capri Room, and allow spatial data and the microphone. Full steps are in Help. Stuck? Ask in the Discord.
