In short

Account
None
Ads
None
Analytics
None in the app
Your words
Stay on your PC, and your phone if you use Capri Remote, unless you pick a cloud service

On your PC

Stays here
Settings, chats, me.md, memories.md, transcripts, reminders, routines and check-ins
Runs here
Her built-in brain, free voice, ears and wake word
Keys
Encrypted for your Windows user
Folder
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Capri, or a private folder Windows gives a fresh Microsoft Store copy
Delete it
Delete that folder. Uninstalling a Store copy removes its private folder. Uninstalling a website install keeps your settings, notes, memories and keys

Capri Remote

Default
Off. Windows only for now
Your traffic
Messages, her replies, screen frames, call audio, her voice and keys you send to your PC are end-to-end encrypted. The relay can't read them
Pairing keys
The pairing key follows # in the link, so GitHub Pages and the relay never get it. Device and session keys stay on your devices
Relay sees
Room and device ids, sign-in tokens, the one-time code, timestamps, online phones, and encrypted frame sizes and timing. Cloudflare sees connection details such as your IP
Relay keeps
Token and code hashes, device ids and timestamps. Removed phones lose their hashes when the PC reaches the relay. Rooms expire after 30 days without the PC
Relay logs
Only errors. Frames are never logged, and Cloudflare's per-request logs are off
Abuse limits
The relay counts connections and pairing changes per IP address for a minute, with Cloudflare's rate limiter. It doesn't save addresses
On your PC
Room id, PC token and each phone's id, key, name and date, encrypted with DPAPI for your Windows user
In your browser
Pairing credentials in IndexedDB; the PC's name and pairing date in localStorage
Phone chats
Saved in localStorage with no timed expiry. Unpairing or removal keeps them. Clear capriai.app site data to erase them
Keys to your PC
Sent end-to-end encrypted, saved with Windows DPAPI like a Mainframe key, and never sent back to your phone
Screen view
Encrypted frames only while a phone watches. A badge shows on your PC while a phone watches or calls
Phone only
Your key is saved in this browser's localStorage on capriai.app and sent straight to your chosen provider with your chat. Saved chats stay here; no relay is used
Phone providers
Anthropic, OpenAI, Google AI, OpenRouter or DeepSeek
Shared storage
Capri Remote shares browser storage with capriai.app's other pages. They run only the site's own scripts, plus YouTube's player on the home page after you press Play

Services you pick

Cloud brain
NVIDIA, Google, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek or a server you add gets your messages, her notes and memories, and any screenshot she looks at
Your screen
She looks when you ask, or when you turn on Watch my screen or her game commentary. Pictures go to the brain you picked
Cloud voice
ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, OpenAI or a server you add gets the words she says. ElevenLabs voice chat also gets your mic audio
Capri code
Your messages pass through our server to Anthropic. We keep the code's hash, budget, spend, expiry, request count and last use, not your messages
Their rules
Each service handles your data under its own privacy policy

Other connections

Mods
The list and files come from GitHub. Download counts and ratings come from our server
Mod stats
A download counts once a day per IP, using a salted hash deleted the next day. A rating is kept with a salted hash of your IP
Downloads
Her brain, voices and ears download from GitHub and Hugging Face
Updates
Website installs ask GitHub for the latest version. A Store copy updates through the Microsoft Store
Songs
Finding a song on Spotify can send its name to DuckDuckGo
Discord
Her status goes through the Discord app on your PC, with words like Plugged in or Gaming. Show on Discord turns it off
Mouse and keys
She uses them on your PC when you ask. Her Security settings pick what she asks about first

This website

Help chat
Your question, the last 6 turns and any screenshot go to Claude or Cloudflare Workers AI. We keep hashed daily counters, not your words. Her Discord help bot works the same way
Counts
Download and star buttons count once a day per IP, using a salted hash deleted the next day
Hosts
GitHub Pages serves the site. Cloudflare runs the help chat, counters and Remote relay

Mod rules

Who makes them
Other people. Each mod shows its maker
Not allowed
Sexual content, hate, slurs, gore, malware, taking keys or data, anything illegal, and other people's work without permission
Code mods
They run on your PC, and only after you say yes. Add the ones you trust
Report one
Open a GitHub issue with the mod's name, or use Help > Report a problem in a Store copy. We check it and take it down if it breaks these rules
