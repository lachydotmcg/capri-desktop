# Phone notifications

Capri Remote can tell your phone when she needs you, even with Remote closed:

- **Her answer** to something you sent from your phone, if you've left Remote by the time she finishes.
- **A job asking to go ahead**, like sending a message or closing an app. In the Android app you can tap Allow or Deny straight from the notification, after unlocking your phone.
- **A job ending** (done or stuck) while you're away from your PC.
- **Reminders** that come due while you're away from your PC.
- **A check-in now and then**, when you've been away from your PC for a while: a little hello from her, at most twice a day, never before 10am or after 9pm. It follows **Check in on me** in her Mainframe, so turning that off stops these too.

"Away" means nobody has touched your PC's keyboard or mouse for 2 minutes. While you're at your PC she tells you there instead, and while Remote is open on your phone you see it in Remote.

## Turning them on

Open Capri Remote's menu, find **Notifications** and turn it on. **Send a test** checks it works.

- **iPhone and iPad (iOS 16.4 or later):** notifications only work in the Home Screen app. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open Capri from there, pair it (Remote's menu has a pairing link for this) and turn Notifications on.
- **Android, in Chrome:** turn it on in Remote and allow notifications.
- **The Capri Android app:** turn it on in the app and allow notifications. Leaving the app shows a quiet "Capri will let you know when she needs you" notification: that keeps her connection open so she can reach you. Tap Stop on it to turn notifications off.

## Privacy

Your PC sends web notifications straight to your phone's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft), encrypted so only your phone can read them. The push service sees when one is sent and how big it is, never the words. Capri's relay isn't involved. The Android app gets them over the same encrypted Remote connection as chat.

Removing a phone from Capri's Mainframe, or unpairing on the phone, stops its notifications.

## Limits

- Your PC has to be on and Capri running.
- Only on Windows for now. Mac and Linux are next.
- The Android app has to stay running in the background. If Android closes it to save battery, notifications stop until you open it again.
