# Her memory

Capri keeps what she remembers about you in plain files on your PC. **Her memory** shows all of it in one place, on your PC and on your phone, and lets you change it.

- **About you:** her notes, from `me.md`. Things you told her to remember, your name, pronouns and what you're into.
- **From your chats:** what she wrote down from your chats, by day, from `memories.md`.
- **Games:** what she worked out about each game she played for you.
- **Chat logs:** every day's chat, kept on your PC. Open a day to read it, or forget it.

## What you can do

- **Edit** any note or memory. She reads the new words the next time you talk.
- **Forget** one, and tap **Undo** if you didn't mean to.
- **Add** something for her to remember.
- **Forget a day** of chat. The lines from that day she hadn't turned into memories yet go too.
- **Forget everything about me** clears her notes, chat memories and chat logs. Her game notes stay unless you forget those too.

Passwords, addresses, phone numbers and other personal details are refused, the same as when you ask her to remember something.

## Where

- **On your PC:** her Mainframe, HABITS, **Open her memories**. **Edit as text** there still opens the file itself.
- **On your phone:** Capri Remote, the **Memory** key under the chat. It needs your PC on and Capri running.

## Privacy

- It all stays in the files on your PC. Nothing new is stored anywhere.
- Your phone reads it over Remote's end-to-end encrypted link, the same as your chats. The relay can't read it.
- Your phone keeps none of it. Nothing is saved in the browser, and it's cleared when you leave the page.
- There are no share links and no export.
- Undo only lasts until Capri closes. It's kept in memory on your PC, never in a file.
