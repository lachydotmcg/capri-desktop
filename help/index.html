Install

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  1. Download
  2. Open Capri-Setup.exe
  3. “Windows protected your PC”? More info → Run anyway
  4. Install Capri
  5. “Installing the local speech model”: her free voice, about 316 MB. Skip gets it later
Admin
not needed, installs for your Windows user
Folder
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Capri
Shortcuts
Start menu, plus desktop if you tick it
Startup
she starts with Windows; turn it off in Settings
Needs
nothing else, not Ollama, not Claude Code

Windows warns because Capri is new and not signed yet. Got Capri from this site or GitHub? Then antivirus alerts like Wacatac, Zusy or MachineLearning/Anomalous are false alarms on a new app. Restore the file and allow it. The code is open on GitHub.

Check your download

  1. Open PowerShell in Downloads, run Get-FileHash Capri-Setup.exe
  2. Match it with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page

Mac preview, macOS 15+

  1. Download Apple Silicon or Intel
  2. Unzip, drag Capri into Applications
  3. Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Capri.app
  4. Open Capri
  5. Install Ollama, run ollama pull gemma4:e4b

Apple hasn't approved (notarized) Capri yet, so macOS calls her “damaged” until you run that line. It only clears the downloaded-from-the-internet flag on Capri. No Dock icon is normal.

Linux preview, x64

  1. Download Capri-linux-x64.AppImage, tick "Allow executing file as program" in its Properties (or run chmod +x Capri-linux-x64.AppImage) and open it. Or download Capri-linux-x64.tar.gz, unpack it and run Capri/Capri
  2. Install Ollama, run ollama pull gemma4:e4b

X11: she sits on windows. Wayland: bottom of the screen only.

Call mode (beta) 0.8.3, Windows

She hears your mic and the call separately. Address your requests to Capri to use her usual tools. Right-click her > Open Capri Mainframe > Settings > VOICE > Call mode (beta). Turning it on doesn't join a call.

Does Zoom work?
yes. Call app offers Auto, Discord, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Signal, Skype, TeamSpeak, Mumble, Steam voice chat and Browser (Google Meet, web calls)
Auto
prefers Discord, then a call app making sound. It stays on the call it's hearing while that app makes sound; another app's ping doesn't pull her away. It never picks Browser or Steam. Pick those yourself. Browser captures sound from all tabs
Join a call
ask her. In Discord she clicks a visible Join button or the server voice channel with people in it. In another app she needs its Join button on screen. She can pass on your message, explain you're away and open the game the call picks
Panel and pill
rings follow each voice, captions show who said what. CC toggles captions; Minimize leaves a pill and Expand call brings it back. Red End call ends Capri's call mode and shows a call-ended card
Friends hear her
Settings > VOICE > Her voice in your mic works only in call mode. Use How to for setup and Her voice in your mic: loudness for her level
Only one line?
Discord: User Settings > Voice & Video > Echo Cancellation: Off and Noise Suppression: None. Both can cut her voice after the first line. In another app, turn off noise filtering if it cuts her off

Why can't my friends make her do things?

Friend requests start off. Right-click her > Open Capri Mainframe > Settings > HABITS > Security. In SECURITY, turn on Friends on a call can ask, you confirm. It works only in call mode.

Only your own mic or typing can say yes. She holds one request at a time for 60 seconds, then it expires. Your usual SECURITY settings still apply after you confirm. A friend's voice through your speakers can't wake her or answer as you, even if it leaks into your mic. Friends can still chat or answer the game or song choice you've left to the call.

Code/Husk Mode 0.8.3

Right-click her > Open Capri Mainframe > Settings > Advanced Settings > BRAIN > Code/Husk Mode. Pick Off, Claude Code, Codex or Both. She installs her hooks with backups and keeps other hooks and settings. Restart the coding app; in Codex, trust her hooks when it asks.

While it works
silent captions for tools, failures and progress. She says when it needs your OK, reacts when it finishes and reads a short answer when its window is elsewhere. Subagents get passive clones
Your approval
answer in the coding app. She never approves anything or types into the coding assistant
Off
stops her listener and removes only her hooks

Who she is

she checks every 3 hours. “Capri update ready” → right-click her → Restart to update
Check now

Package managers
“Update through winget.” means winget installed her, so update with winget. The same goes for Scoop, Chocolatey and pacman
Mac, Linux
she says when a new version is out. Right-click her → Get Capri X, download it and replace the old one
What's new
the main page changelog, GitHub releases, the Discord

Uninstall

  1. Right-click her → Quit Capri
  2. Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Capri → Uninstall
  3. Remove Capri

Your settings, notes, personality and chat stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Capri. Delete that folder to remove everything. Used voice chat? Delete the “Capri” agent in ElevenLabs too.

Fixes

Install

“Item Not Found”, or the installer vanished
Chrome or Edge: Ctrl + J → Keep. Antivirus: restore it from quarantine. Open it from Downloads.
Antivirus flags it
From this site or GitHub? A false alarm on a new, unsigned app. Restore and allow it, or check the download.
Capri vanished after installing
Your antivirus removed Capri.exe. Restore it, allow it, run the installer again.
Opera: “Insufficient permissions”
Use Chrome or Edge, or download from GitHub.
“Quit Capri from her tray menu, then install again.”
Right-click her → Quit Capri, then install.
Mac: “damaged” or “can't be opened”
Run the xattr line, then open her again. Needs macOS 15+.
Mac or Linux: no Mainframe
Capri Mainframe is included in 0.8.3. Update if you're on an older preview.
Linux: brain offline
Run ollama pull gemma4:e4b, then right-click her → Brain.

Brain

“My brain's offline.”
Mainframe → Set up her brain, or Free key.
Download keeps stopping
Check your internet → Try again. It picks up where it stopped.
“Brain crashed loading” or “loaded too slowly”
Close games → Refresh. Or use the free NVIDIA key.
Slow replies
Games share your PC's power. Use the free NVIDIA key.
“Waiting for NVIDIA”
Pick another model, like nvidia/nemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b. It's faster, but can't see your screen.
“Key rejected.”
Forget, copy the key again, paste it → Connect.
“No credits on this key.”
OpenAI, Claude and DeepSeek need paid credits. Or use Free key.
“That model isn't on your key.”
Pick another model.
“My brain sent gibberish.” or <unused49>
She retries on your CPU by herself. Keeps happening? Use the free NVIDIA key.
She forgets what you said
Mainframe Settings → Memory → Long, and keep Remember our chats on.
She makes things up
Small brains on your PC guess. The free NVIDIA key is smarter.
PC freezes
Try the free NVIDIA key, then tell us in the Discord.

Voice

“ElevenLabs rejected the key”
That isn't the key. Keys start with sk_. Make a new one and paste it.
“Key is missing permissions” or “Key can't do that”
Make a new key with Restrict key turned off. Forget the old one, paste the new one.
“Voice not found”
Paste the Voice ID from My Voices, not the name. Library voices go in My Voices first.
“Out of ElevenLabs credits”
Credits refill each month. Pick PREBUILT LOCAL or Fish Audio meanwhile. “10,000 / 10,000” means all used.
“ElevenLabs has restricted your account”
Sign in at elevenlabs.io to see why. Pick PREBUILT LOCAL meanwhile.
“Free tier usage disabled”
Turn off your VPN and try again.
“ElevenLabs sent a reply Capri couldn't read.”
ElevenLabs may be blocked where you are, or by a VPN. Try without one, then send Copy error details.
Test shows words, no sound
“Voice link online” isn't proof she played. Set your speakers as the Windows default (Settings → System → Sound). Check Mainframe Settings → Volume. Quit and reopen Capri.
PREBUILT LOCAL, but she only types
Her free voice isn't installed. Get free voice under VOICE.
“Can't open your speakers”
Turn on speakers in Settings → System → Sound. Or close the app holding them, or untick exclusive mode.
“Can't reach your voice server”
Start your server, then check its address under CUSTOM LOCAL → Set up. Her free voice fills in meanwhile.
“Fish Audio wants credit”
For its free tier, set Model to s2.1-pro-free.

Voice chat

She can't hear you
Pick your mic under VOICE. Check it isn't muted. Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → let desktop apps use it.
“Can't open my voice link.”
Open the Mainframe. The reason and the fix are under VOICE.
Free voice chat when you want ElevenLabs
Pick CLOUD VOICE → ElevenLabs. Voice chat follows your VOICE pick.
“My ears are still downloading”
First time only. She says when she can hear you.
“Voice chat needs my Voice ID”
Pick a voice in the Voice ID box.
Ctrl + Alt + V does nothing
Another app may use it. Click the hint in the Mainframe and pick new keys.

Screen and Mainframe

She never comments
Settings → Watch my screen. She skips screens that haven't changed.
“Couldn't read the screen.”
Most NVIDIA models can't see. Pick nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b.
“This brain can't see.”
Use the free NVIDIA key.
“The screen capture is black.”
Set the game to borderless or windowed.
Mainframe squashed or cut off
Try Windows Settings → System → Display → Scale → 100%, reopen Capri, and tell us your scale in the Discord.

Moving her

She's gone
Double-click her tray icon (maybe under the ^), press Ctrl + Alt + V, or open Capri again.
Stuck on your cursor
Ctrl + Alt + C.
She won't wander
Turn on Wander around, use your PC for a bit, give it a couple of minutes. Not from a Sit here spot. Needs windows that aren't maximized.
Floating in mid-air
Move → Sit on windows.
Behind your game
Set the game to borderless or windowed. Settings → Always on top.
She doesn't dance
Turn on Dance to music. Give her a few seconds to find the beat.

Doing things

Wrong song
Say the song and the artist.
Spotify opens but doesn't play
Press the green Play button.
“No app called X is installed”
Use the name from your Start menu.
“Mute my mic” doesn't work
She uses Discord's default keys (Ctrl + Shift + M). Set them back in Discord.
Jobs go wrong
Pick a bigger model, or a Claude key. Esc stops her.
She won't play a song
Some words in song names are blocked. Play it yourself.

Updates

“Windows couldn't verify the update signature.”
Download the latest Capri-Setup.exe and install it. Don't get around the check.
Old version won't update
Install the latest Capri-Setup.exe over it.
