Install

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  1. Download
  2. Open Capri-Setup.exe
  3. “Windows protected your PC”? More info → Run anyway
  4. Install Capri
  5. “Installing the local speech model”: her free voice, about 316 MB. Skip gets it later
Admin
not needed, installs for your Windows user
Folder
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Capri
Shortcuts
Start menu, plus desktop if you tick it
Startup
she starts with Windows; turn it off in Settings
Needs
nothing else, not Ollama, not Claude Code

Windows warns because Capri is new and not signed yet. Got Capri from this site or GitHub? Then antivirus alerts like Wacatac, Zusy or MachineLearning/Anomalous are false alarms on a new app. Restore the file and allow it. The code is open on GitHub.

Check your download

  1. Open PowerShell in Downloads, run Get-FileHash Capri-Setup.exe
  2. Match it with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page

Mac preview, macOS 15+

  1. Download Apple Silicon or Intel
  2. Unzip, drag Capri into Applications
  3. Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Capri.app
  4. Open Capri
  5. Install Ollama, run ollama pull gemma4:e4b

Apple hasn't approved (notarized) Capri yet, so macOS calls her “damaged” until you run that line. It only clears the downloaded-from-the-internet flag on Capri. No Dock icon is normal.

Linux preview, x64

  1. Download Capri-linux-x64.tar.gz, unpack it
  2. Run Capri/Capri
  3. Install Ollama, run ollama pull gemma4:e4b

X11: she sits on windows. Wayland: bottom of the screen only.

Free NVIDIA key

  1. Mainframe → Free key, or build.nvidia.com
  2. Sign in or make a free account. NVIDIA may ask to verify your phone
  3. Generate API Key, copy it. It starts with nvapi-
  4. Pick HIGHER, paste it in the box → Connect

She starts on nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b. It can see your screen. Most NVIDIA models can't. If a model doesn't answer, she switches to another for that reply. Your pick stays.

RAM and storage

“Not enough free RAM”
Close apps, browser tabs or games, then try again. Or use the free NVIDIA key.
“Your storage is full”
Free up space on C: or empty the Recycle Bin, then Set up her brain again.
Games lag
Use the free NVIDIA key while you play.

Her voice free, your own model, or a cloud voice

Our project-provided synthetic voice is in Capri's Mainframe and runs on your PC. You may connect third-party text-to-speech services, but we don't provide guides for voice cloning tools. Our only voice-design help is a text prompt for making a synthetic voice. Only use voices you have the necessary rights and permissions to use, and follow the provider's terms. We do not endorse unauthorized cloning or impersonation of voice actors or other individuals. Capri prohibits cloning or impersonating a living person's voice without explicit, informed consent or other valid authorization.

Watch my screen

  1. Mainframe → Settings → Watch my screen
  2. Every: how often she looks

Any time: say “check my screen” and she looks right then.

She remembers what she saw for 3 hours, in text and voice chat, so “what did you see?” gets a real answer. Keep her caught up (on by default) adds the glances she keeps quiet about.

She skips unchanged, blank or loading screens, and stays quiet in voice chat or after 10 minutes away. Qwen3 4B, DeepSeek and most NVIDIA models can't see. Use the free NVIDIA key with nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b.

Who she is

Her personality and safety rules are fixed in her code, the same Capri for everyone, so since 0.7.6 there's no personality.md to edit. Her safety rules are part of who she is: games, songs and jokes are make-believe so she plays along, she talks you out of real-life risks and points you to a helpline if you need one. If you build on her, please keep them as they are. What she calls you, her language and her voice are yours to pick in Settings.

  1. Right-click her → Quit Capri
  2. Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Capri → Uninstall
  3. Remove Capri

Your settings, notes, personality and chat stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Capri. Delete that folder to remove everything. Used voice chat? Delete the “Capri” agent in ElevenLabs too.

Fixes

Install

“Item Not Found”, or the installer vanished
Chrome or Edge: Ctrl + J → Keep. Antivirus: restore it from quarantine. Open it from Downloads.
Antivirus flags it
From this site or GitHub? A false alarm on a new, unsigned app. Restore and allow it, or check the download.
Capri vanished after installing
Your antivirus removed Capri.exe. Restore it, allow it, run the installer again.
Opera: “Insufficient permissions”
Use Chrome or Edge, or download from GitHub.
“Quit Capri from her tray menu, then install again.”
Right-click her → Quit Capri, then install.
Mac: “damaged” or “can't be opened”
Run the xattr line, then open her again. Needs macOS 15+.
Mac or Linux: no Mainframe
There isn't one yet. Right-click her.
Linux: brain offline
Run ollama pull gemma4:e4b, then right-click her → Brain.

Brain

“My brain's offline.”
Mainframe → Set up her brain, or Free key.
Download keeps stopping
Check your internet → Try again. It picks up where it stopped.
“Brain crashed loading” or “loaded too slowly”
Close games → Refresh. Or use the free NVIDIA key.
Slow replies
Games share your PC's power. Use the free NVIDIA key.
“Waiting for NVIDIA”
Pick another model, like nvidia/nemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b. It's faster, but can't see your screen.
“Key rejected.”
Forget, copy the key again, paste it → Connect.
“No credits on this key.”
OpenAI, Claude and DeepSeek need paid credits. Or use Free key.
“That model isn't on your key.”
Pick another model.
“My brain sent gibberish.” or <unused49>
She retries on your CPU by herself. Keeps happening? Use the free NVIDIA key.
She forgets what you said
Mainframe Settings → Memory → Long, and keep Remember our chats on.
She makes things up
Small brains on your PC guess. The free NVIDIA key is smarter.
PC freezes
Try the free NVIDIA key, then tell us in the Discord.

Voice

“ElevenLabs rejected the key”
That isn't the key. Keys start with sk_. Make a new one and paste it.
“Key is missing permissions” or “Key can't do that”
Make a new key with Restrict key turned off. Forget the old one, paste the new one.
“Voice not found”
Paste the Voice ID from My Voices, not the name. Library voices go in My Voices first.
“Out of ElevenLabs credits”
Credits refill each month. Pick PREBUILT LOCAL or Fish Audio meanwhile. “10,000 / 10,000” means all used.
“ElevenLabs has restricted your account”
Sign in at elevenlabs.io to see why. Pick PREBUILT LOCAL meanwhile.
“Free tier usage disabled”
Turn off your VPN and try again.
“ElevenLabs sent a reply Capri couldn't read.”
ElevenLabs may be blocked where you are, or by a VPN. Try without one, then send Copy error details.
Test shows words, no sound
“Voice link online” isn't proof she played. Set your speakers as the Windows default (Settings → System → Sound). Check Mainframe Settings → Volume. Quit and reopen Capri.
PREBUILT LOCAL, but she only types
Her free voice isn't installed. Get free voice under VOICE.
“Can't open your speakers”
Turn on speakers in Settings → System → Sound. Or close the app holding them, or untick exclusive mode.
“Can't reach your voice server”
Start your server, then check its address under CUSTOM LOCAL → Set up. Her free voice fills in meanwhile.
“Fish Audio wants credit”
For its free tier, set Model to s2.1-pro-free.
